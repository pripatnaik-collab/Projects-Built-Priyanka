BUSINESS EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Report on Analysing Customer Orders Using Python
1.	Customer Analysis
A list of six customers namely Ravi, Sargun, Geeta, Sita, Sourabh and Varun have been considered. Their respective orders have been stored as tuples in-order of customer name, product, price, category inside a list. The products are categorised into three different categories Electronics, Clothing, HomeEssentials. These details are stored inside a dictionary as key-value pairs and have been displayed. Using dictionary methods values, keys, items the respective orders along with customer names have been displayed.
2.	Objective and Method 
Use of sets, dictionaries, tuples and lists has been done. Different dictionary methods, conversion to lists, sets, operators of sets have been done effectively over the customer orders. A brief summary of the customer orders has been done where a summary of each customer including the customer names, total spends by the customer, classification of the customer has been done. Revenue across each category, the top three customers, the most popular category along with the most popular items which are purchased by the customers has also been effectively displayed.
3.	Findings
On analysis of the customer orders, the top three customers (Sourabh, Geeta, Ravi) based on their spending patterns have been displayed using sorting. Use of list comprehension has been done to find all the customers who purchased from ‘Electronics’ category. The revenue for each of the categories has also been displayed. Use of functions has been applied and the revenue for ‘Electronics’ is Rs.1000, revenue for ‘Clothing’ is Rs.1400 and revenue for ‘HomeEssentials’ is 1300 for a random sample of data which has been considered. Set operations have been used to find customers who have purchased from multiple categories. Based on the number of customers the most popular category has been identified and displayed using if-else (conditional statements). HomeEssentials is the most popular category and the items ‘Sofa’ and ‘Bed’ in HomeEssentials are the most popularly purchased by the customers.
4.	Conclusion
The entire report shows the analysis of customer orders displaying different categories, the items under each category. Based on the spending trends of the customers, the customers are classified as high-value, moderate or low-value buyers. The top three customers have also been displayed based on the customers spending patterns. A detailed summary based on customers spending patterns and customer classification have also been displayed using functions. The revenue for each category has been displayed. The customer orders have been further analysed to understand the customers spending trends based on the purchases made from multiple categories. The most popular category and the products have also been displayed. The report demonstrates the use of Python in multiple fields to display the results as desired as per current market trends.
